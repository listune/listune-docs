# Listune Discord Music Bot

#### High-quality music, powerful filters, and a real-time web dashboard.
Listune is a professional Discord music bot built for stability, speed, and ease of use. It is 100% free with no features locked behind paywalls or voting requirements.

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Multi-Platform Support:** Play music from YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud, Apple Music, Deezer, Tidal, and more.
- **Web Dashboard:** Control your music in real-time through a beautiful web interface.
- **Audio Filters:** Access 25+ real-time filters including Bass Boost, Nightcore, Vaporwave, and 3D audio.
- **Music Trivia:** Challenge your friends with an interactive music guessing game.
- **Personal Playlists:** Create and manage your own playlists directly within Discord.
- **24/7 Mode:** Keep the music playing around the clock without interruptions.
- **Last.fm Integration:** Automatically log your listening history to your Last.fm profile.
- **Localizations:** Support for over 20 languages to serve communities worldwide.

## 🛠️ Getting Started

1. [**Invite Listune**](https://listune.app/invite) to your Discord server.
2. Join a voice channel.
3. Type `/play` followed by a song name or URL.
4. Enjoy the music!
