# Welcome to Listune

Listune is a feature-rich Discord music bot designed to bring high-quality audio playback directly into your server. Whether you are hosting a listening party, running background music for a community hangout, or competing in a music trivia game, Listune has you covered.

## Completely Free

Every single feature in Listune is free to use. There are no paywalls, no locked features, and no premium tiers. Everyone gets full access to everything, from audio filters and playlists to 24/7 mode and bot customization.

## What Can Listune Do?

Listune is built for music lovers. Here is a quick overview of what makes it special:

- **Play music from multiple platforms.** Search and play tracks from YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud, Apple Music, Deezer, Tidal, and more. Just paste a link or type a song name.
- **Real-time Web Dashboard.** Control playback, manage the queue, and configure server settings from a beautiful web dashboard that syncs live with Discord.
- **Audio Filters.** Apply over 25 real-time audio effects like Nightcore, Bass Boost, Vaporwave, Karaoke, and more to customize your listening experience.
- **Playlists.** Create, manage, and share personal playlists directly through Discord. Save your current queue to a playlist with a single command.
- **Music Trivia.** Challenge your friends with an interactive music guessing game. Listune plays song clips and everyone races to identify the track.
- **Lyrics.** View synchronized or plain lyrics for the currently playing song, right inside Discord.
- **Last.fm Scrobbling.** Connect your Last.fm account to automatically log every track you listen to through Listune.
- **Favorites and History.** Like songs during playback to build a personal favorites list. Review your listening history and replay tracks at any time.
- **Server Customization.** Set a DJ role, configure a dedicated song request channel, change the bot language, and personalize the bot profile per server.
- **24/7 Mode.** Keep Listune connected to a voice channel around the clock so the music never stops.
